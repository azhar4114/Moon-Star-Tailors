Our Heritage

38+ Years of Master Tailoring in Rasoolpura, Begumpet

Established in 1988, Moon Star Tailors is a long-standing family-run men's tailoring shop situated in Rasoolpura, Begumpet, Hyderabad. For nearly four decades, our master tailors have been dedicated to the fine art of cutting and fitting men's clothing.

We have built our reputation on dependable craftsmanship, fair pricing, and personal attention to every client. Whether stitching everyday formal shirts and trousers for working professionals or fulfilling custom uniform contracts for local businesses, schools, and hospitality establishments, our commitment remains consistent: garments that fit well, feel comfortable, and endure daily use.
