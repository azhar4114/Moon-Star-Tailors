From a Single Sewing Machine to Hyderabad's Most Trusted Tailor

In 1988, with nothing but a passion for perfection and a single sewing machine, our founder opened the doors of Moon Star Tailors in the heart of Hyderabad. What started as a modest shop has grown into a legacy trusted by generations of families.

For nearly four decades, we've been crafting more than garments — we've been crafting confidence. Every stitch carries the weight of our reputation, every measurement reflects our commitment to the perfect fit.

From the groom's first sherwani to the executive's power suit, from the student's first uniform to the father's festive kurta — Moon Star Tailors has been there for every milestone.

"A well-tailored garment doesn't just fit the body — it fits the soul."

— Founder, Moon Star Tailors
